> **Note:** The application form is in Swedish. This residency is open to producers and artists based in Västra Götaland, Sweden.

### Apply for a residency at one of Western Sweden's most renowned studios

The Cultural Department of the Västra Götaland Region is offering a residency at Strawberry Studio in Töreboda – a legendary studio with an outstanding track record of professional recordings.

The residency is aimed at producers and artists active in Västra Götaland who want to take their music to the next level in a professional studio environment.

### What's included?

* Access to Strawberry Studio's fully equipped facilities during the residency period
* Mentoring opportunities from experienced music producers
* Networking with other creatives in the region

### Who can apply?

The open call is directed at professional or semi-professional music producers and artists based in Västra Götaland.

### How to apply

Complete the application form via the link below. Include your artistic project, a short biography and contact details. The application deadline is **15 May 2026**.
