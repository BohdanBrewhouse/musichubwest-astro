### The Swedish music industry meets the German one

Reeperbahn Festival in Hamburg is one of Europe's most important industry gatherings. Export Music Sweden is there this autumn and is arranging two things on **17 September**: a matchmaking session between the Swedish and German music industries, followed by a networking event for the Swedish and international industry at Wiener Bistro at Arcotel at 16:00.

The initiative is run in cooperation with the Swedish Embassy in Berlin.

### Two separate things to apply for

**Travel support** — a limited number of places. If you are accepted, a place at the matchmaking session is included, and attending it is mandatory.

**Discount code for a delegate pass** — unlimited places. If you only want the discount on the pass, you are not competing for a place.

### Who can apply?

The support is aimed at business owners, people employed by companies, songwriters and composers, and artists and performers.

**Please note:** ExMS cannot award travel support to organisations, or to people employed by organisations whose principal is a region, a municipality or the state. The same applies if your primary income comes from a region, a municipality or the state.

### Practical details

* Location: Hamburg, Germany
* Matchmaking and networking event: 17 September 2026
* Networking event: Wiener Bistro, Arcotel, 16:00
* Applications are handled by ExMS and require an ExMS Business login

The application deadline is stated on the application page.

The application page and the call are in Swedish.
