### Have you got an idea or a business you want to take further?

This autumn we're launching a new programme for you who work in or around the music industry in Västra Götaland – artist, creator, someone running a business, or on the way to building something of your own.

Maybe you recognise yourself in one of these questions:

* **Do you need to reach a new audience?**
* **Do you want to find new partners to collaborate with?**
* **Are you wondering how to develop or broaden what you offer?**
* **Have you got an idea you want to test or take further?**

If you're nodding along, this is for you.

### What is it?

We meet five times and work hands-on with your business and your ideas. You'll get tools, support and fresh perspectives – and meet others facing similar questions. The focus is on making real progress, at your own pace.

* Five sessions of three hours each
* Starts: 1 October, 15:00
* Application deadline: Friday 18 September
* Location: Brewhouse, Åvägen 24, Gothenburg
* Free to take part

### Who is it for?

The programme is for you who are active in the music industry in Västra Götaland and want to grow. You don't need to have everything figured out – an idea or the will to take the next step is enough. This time we're especially reaching out to those we haven't met before.

### How to register

Registration is open now – the application deadline is Friday 18 September. Fill in the form and we'll get back to you with more information. Places are limited.

Questions? Get in touch at [hello@musichubwest.se](mailto:hello@musichubwest.se).
