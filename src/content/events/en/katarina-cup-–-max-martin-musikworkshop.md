During the spring of 2026, KATARINA CUP has hosted a series of music workshops across the country. Some of the most influential figures in Swedish culture have joined us to share their creative processes, expert tips, and lives in music.

Now, we are concluding this year’s initiative with the biggest legend of them all: MAX MARTIN. Come and listen to the conversation at KATARINA CUP on May 30th.

The session is presented by STIM.
