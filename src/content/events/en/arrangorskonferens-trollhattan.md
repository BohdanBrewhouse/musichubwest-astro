> **Note:** This conference is held in Swedish.

### A full-day conference for cultural organisers in Västra Götaland

Subkultfestivalen and the Cultural Department of the Västra Götaland Region invite organisers, festivals and cultural actors from across the region to a full-day conference.

Participants will share experiences, discuss challenges and explore opportunities in the regional cultural landscape.

### The programme includes:

* Keynote on audience development and reaching new demographics
* Workshops on sustainable event organisation and funding models
* Panel discussion with experienced festival organisers from the region
* Networking session and shared lunch

### Who is this conference for?

The conference is aimed at organisers, festivals, cultural associations and other actors involved in cultural events in Västra Götaland – whether you are just starting out or a seasoned organiser.

Registration is via the Västra Götaland Region event calendar. Spaces are limited.
