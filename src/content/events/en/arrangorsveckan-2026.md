### A full week for anyone organising culture in Västra Götaland

The Culture Administration of Västra Götalandsregionen invites you to Arrangörsveckan 2026 — six days of free seminars, masterclasses and conversations for those working practically or strategically with organising culture.

The week is built so that you **pick freely from the programme**. Some sessions take place in Gothenburg, others online — and each one has its own registration.

### Why it matters for the music industry

We've highlighted the sessions closest to those working with music: funding, audience development, booking acts, and the practical side of **STIM, copyright and screening rights** when you put on live music.

Sessions marked ★ are the ones we think give the most if you work in music.

### Good to know

- All sessions are free of charge
- Registration is made separately for each session, via the region's event calendar
- The full programme has 19 sessions — below is a selection. See the organiser's page for everything.
- Note: sessions are held in Swedish
