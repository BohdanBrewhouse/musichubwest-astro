### New Gothenburg artists live at Grönsakstorget

During Göteborgskalaset, Musiklabbet takes over the stage at Grönsakstorget with four new bands and artists from Gothenburg. This is music you haven't heard played to death yet — live and up close.

Musiklabbet exists to give artists early in their careers the chance to develop by performing for an audience. On Saturday, four acts take turns on stage between 12:00 and 17:00.

### Good to know

* Free entry — no booking required
* The stage is at Grönsakstorget; the nearest stop is Grönsakstorget
* Göteborgskalaset is organised by Göteborg & Co on behalf of the City of Gothenburg
* Musiklabbet's programme is put together with the study associations, including ABF, Studieförbundet Bilda Sydväst and Medborgarskolan
